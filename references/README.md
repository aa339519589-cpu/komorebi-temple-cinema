# Visual references

The newest full-resolution images in [hd/](hd/) are now the primary visual references, as requested by the user. The original ten small collage crops remain at `01.png` through `10.png` for historical comparison.

The new upload contains nine JPEG files. All nine are retained with their original filenames. They show the sunset veranda, lotus pond and bridge, frontal approach, Buddha sanctuary, pagoda eaves, garden overview, framed passage, night scene and rainy courtyard.

These are user-supplied reference images, not runtime textures or models. They are not included in the code's MIT license grant. Architectural reconstruction continues to use code-generated geometry and materials.
