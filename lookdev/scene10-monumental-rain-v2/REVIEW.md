This lane owns only reconstruction 10, QA 10 and this lookdev directory. It rebuilt actual architecture, terrain, receivers, plants and wet surfaces through seven native review iterations. R07 is held at `7c4dd0afb6c113fdebec07b57b566283666110a026e794b8e49f17579c66567d`; visual acceptance remains false.

R03 introduced real curved botanical leaf meshes and a closed blind column receiver, and directed the low opening camera toward the temple. R06 aligned the recessed shrine to its support and fitted the actual drainage line. The true R06 ground subfrustum then revealed that the closest courses still lacked the detailed solids/joint beds and that fallen leaves were oversized. R07 extended those bodies through the foreground, placed smaller cupped leaves on actual surface heights, and corrected physical source flux and probe completeness.

The inspected R07 full frame reads as a 3D rainy temple, with a more source-sized tower, a supported upper gallery, distinct bridge paint, actual scene reflections and smaller grounded leaves. The true ground capture shows real spalls, pits and narrowed shallow joints. It still falls well short of the supplied photograph. The native hall has orderly, simplified bearing rows and insufficient carved mass. The grove is sparse and needle-like, and the hill retains exposed soil. The lantern and paving edges are too regular. The entrance does not yet read as the deep gilded room in HD10, and native illumination/reflection contrast still differs materially. These are next reconstruction priorities, not accepted deviations.
