# Scene03 closed-bud placement and tissue98

The preceding real bud projected to x−68..119, y954..1343 in the 1254px
reference camera. Much of it was off frame. The supplied photo has a smaller,
right-leaning closed bud around x54..156, y970..1150, with approximately 8px
uncertainty because this foreground object is blurred.

`fit-bud.mjs` projects the actual closed bud geometry using the fixed scene
camera. It retains depth z16.45, sets an authored lean of −.26rad about worldZ,
then fits x/y/uniform scale to that observed silhouette's centre and height.
This is a 2D landmark fit of real geometry. It does not recover metric depth,
and no reference RGB supplies a texture, transparency mask or rendered frame.

The longitudinal diffuse and shallow tissue bump in `lotus-bud98.js` are
authored code. Five petal-overlap traces, a green lower calyx transitioning to
rose, and fine tissue break the former uniformly pink surface. These are
botanical hypotheses rather than measurements of the blurred photograph.

98a actual full/detail were inspected. The stem was too thick and its top cap
was exposed at the pinched attachment. 98b uses a 4.8mm radius stalk whose end
extends 25mm into the closed bud, retaining actual contact. No garden anchors,
random draws, other petals/leaves, architecture, light or camera were changed.
The complete scene and the botanical sculptural fidelity remain unaccepted.
