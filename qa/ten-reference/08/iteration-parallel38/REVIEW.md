# Scene 08 — bounded architecture and daylight revision

Viewed the supplied HD photograph and integrated-02 actual comparison before editing. No camera movement; the close veranda, left halls, tower, bridge and garden remain actual geometry.

Source anchors addressed: the photograph has thin blue-gray ceramic eaves with deep red/gold timber support, compact red/white tower storeys, a visible tiled top crown, and a slender multi-ring finial. This revision thins the orange fascia, adds repeated curved bracket cornices to the halls, rebuilds the tower elevations and balconies, and replaces its broad wire-like finial with a narrow tapered core and 23 rings. Warm side sunlight and cooler fill replace the uniformly yellow-green lighting.

The first actual 1254px frame is preserved in `iteration-parallel37`; after viewing it, one correction restores shadow brightness and raises the top roof slope so its blue-gray tile field is visible. `iteration-parallel38/reference.png` and its report correspond to the final scene source identity. Both full frames were rendered through the shared GPU queue. No synthetic or broad test suite was run.

The final actual image was inspected. The tower crown, supporting cornices and thinner eaves read more clearly, but the reconstruction remains visibly below the supplied photograph: the garden and lantern remain coarse, the long side elevation is too regular, and roof/wall proportions and the foreground framing are still estimates. Neither candidate is accepted or an identical match.
