## Modeled changes

- Replaced the glossy upper facade slab and oversized detached-looking block assemblies with closed curved timber arms, three bearing courses, actual upper round posts at approximately 2.6m bay pitch, recessed cream infill and continuous receiving members. A concealed sill and short supports transfer the upper posts into the lower framing. The original hall roof silhouettes and lower reference-bearing axes remain.
- Added real gilt end fittings and cast floral relief, modeled carved bay arches, a taller connected portal frame, recessed gilded sanctuary panels and actual paper-backed window frames. Hall timber has a distinct aged finish. Scanned wood roughness was raised, clearcoat lowered and member-aligned grain retained.
- Widened the bridge toward the photograph’s left bank, changed its rail spacing, shifted its central bent left and raised its stone bearing to shorten the exposed wood leg. Both ends retain masonry abutments. A stepped masonry connector turns back through the planted bank toward the hall approach.
- Seated rock and plant placements on the continuous bank. Added actual rounded broadleaf tree crowns, mixed understory and overlapping rock shelves. The existing rooted maple has drooping branchlets and a bark/lichen field. Individual leaves, stems and branches remain modeled geometry.
- Moved the stone lantern into its photographed position. It now has crystalline granite material, a closed irregular stone roof, modeled lotus-bud finial, corner piers and actual interlocking wood lattice. Granite maps are runtime DataTextures made from seed132219; there is no reference-image projection.
- Recovered the stream’s farther bank position. Its two lips, cut channel, rock beds, foam position and falling ribbons use the same layout data. Ribbons have staggered phases and lower opacity. Pond reflections come from the real scene with stronger ripple distortion and roughness filtering.
- Corrected the lotus pad vertical scaling exposed by the genuine pond crop. Pads are now shallow cupped leaves, with overlapping pads, modeled petal rings and stems. Their wet-leaf clearcoat/specular response was reduced so they remain green.
- Rebuilt the moss coating as a welded closed surface following scanned stone positions/normals. The rejected r08 per-triangle prisms produced 72.7M effective triangles. The selected welded version preserves cap normals and gives 50,095,707 effective scene triangles, 566,367 instances. No source decimation was used.
- Retained the independently generated sky-only panorama and physical Sun direction. The source now explicitly records a gain3.5 radiance proxy, 10% luminance mixing and `[.97,1,1.08]` linear color gains. The camera sky gain is .40; the same texture supplies reflected radiance. This is authored radiance, not a measured HDRI. The old Cycles study describes the old source and is not an equivalent render of this checkpoint.

## Verification and remaining gaps

The final full/detail pair, all three pose frames and the above view have zero reported resource/console errors, GL error0, linked programs and valid nonempty framebuffers. All receipt source hashes and the live source hash were checked equal. Syntax and whitespace checks passed for the edited scene source. These checks establish a working render, not fidelity.

The largest remaining gaps are the regular, comparatively broad carpentry/infill rhythm, uniform ceramic finish, overly regular shrub crowns/flower distribution and comparatively simple moss/rock transitions. The water still has a stylized native shader response. The plaque strokes are only an unverified ornament proxy. The opening and above inspection expose comparatively sparse peripheral terrain and the finite modeled footprint. The above view also makes the close pagoda/hall roof relationship apparent; that construction needs further source-led review before claiming complete architectural fidelity. Foreground metal and shadowed wood still differ from the photograph’s fine, bright, warm response.

The first rejected pass’s bare bridge soil cones are retained in `iteration-scene01-solmax-bearing-and-planted-banks-r01-20261021`. Later genuine-detail failures and their source snapshots are also retained. The current source is held for root review; it is not frozen as an accepted final scene.
