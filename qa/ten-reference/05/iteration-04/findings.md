# Fourth render: rejected framing diagnosis

The real fourth render has no GL error. It still exposes the tower foundation and too many compressed eaves. Full-resolution comparison shows that the photograph's dominant eave belongs to a higher storey; treating it as the lowest roof forces a shallow angle and exposes the footings.

Revision five keeps the camera physically at 1.02 m and aims at the next storey. Six original landmarks (main eave corner, far eave corner, upper eave corners, pillar head, railing finial) guide the projection. Layer spacing is 4.75 m, and the lens is narrower. The waist eave and carved inserts repeat across all storeys, rather than being attached only to the ground storey. This is a geometric/camera candidate requiring an actual render; no photographic acceptance is asserted.
