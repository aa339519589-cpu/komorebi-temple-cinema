# Low pagoda-corner framing repair

Photographic acceptance remains false.

The near corner was too central and the two visible tower faces had the wrong balance. The camera remains 0.8m above ground; its position changes from [12.5,0.8,13.7] to [10.8,0.8,13.7], aim from [-1.5,16,0] to [-1.7,15,0], and vertical field of view from 33.5 to 35.5 degrees. Two actual native inspection-camera candidates were compared before changing the canonical reference view.

The seven-second dolly uses the same near subject and now reaches the revised physical camera/aim continuously. This is a camera reconstruction hypothesis, not an exact recovered source lens.

The full native frame and detail subfrustum retain all actual roof, bracket, lantern and foliage geometry. The timber brackets remain too broad and regular, ceramic and metal carving remain simpler than the photo, and canopy/material response still differ strongly. The camera change does not establish modeled-detail fidelity.
