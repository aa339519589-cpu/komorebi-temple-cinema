# Fifth render: upper-tier framing

The fifth actual render removes the foundation naturally with a ground-level camera and recovers the main eave/pillar/rail composition. It remains unaccepted. The rafter spacing is now visibly too tight compared with the original, the capital volume is too thin, and ambient lighting fails to occlude the many real joints.

Revision six restores 0.245 m rafter spacing with stronger physical sections, increases the seated capital assembly to 0.90 scale, and adds GTAO calculated from actual scene depth and normals. This is a synchronous 3D render/composite ending in the default framebuffer, with no source-image input. Wood joint contacts, timber cavities and bracket layering are the target of the contact pass; the new render must be checked for halos or excessive darkening.
