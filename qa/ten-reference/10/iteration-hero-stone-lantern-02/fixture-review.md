# Hero stone lantern geometry review

`reference.png` is a native Metal render at 1254×1254. `detail-native.png` is a second, genuine 1254×1254 camera subfrustum render from crop `[160, 410, 520, 1254]`; `lantern-crop-comparison.png` places that close view beside the corresponding reference-photo crop. The render receipt reports both frames valid, zero GL errors, and `visualAccepted: false`.

The fixture now has a broad chamfered granite plinth, a flat-faceted shaft and capital, raised stone bands with incised joints, thick stone corner piers around four recessed lit openings, a solid curled square hip roof, connected surface moss patches, and a rough black-granite giboshi. Its anchor, base, overall height, chamber-light position, camera, and surrounding scene are unchanged. The fixture consumes the previous moss-placement random calls so later garden placement remains stable.

The repaired silhouette and finial are closer to the reference, and the eaves read as darker stone. Remaining gaps: the stone still lacks the photograph’s fine chipped/weathered relief; at the full camera view the lit apertures read as square glowing panels; most roof moss is hidden by the low viewing angle; and the full reconstruction remains visibly less dense and photographic than the source. This is a fixture refinement, not a scene match.
