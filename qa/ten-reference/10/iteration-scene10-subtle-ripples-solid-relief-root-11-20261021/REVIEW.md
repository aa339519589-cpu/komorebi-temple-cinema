# Rain temple solid relief and compact roof

Visual acceptance remains false. Layout, sculpture, foliage and wood construction still differ substantially from the reference.

The excessive 7.7 m upper-roof rise is reduced to 5.2 m. One coupled definition drives roof skin, rafter undersides, header and bracket bearing heights, so the revised pitch does not separate those assemblies. The low camera, bridge, grounded stair and foundation positions are preserved.

The existing generated lotus-karakusa sculpting guide is converted to a scalar field and then to actual closed relief volumes. Cut-out interstices show the dark timber behind. These meshes replace the simple loop/flower symbols in the hall friezes; they receive the real lighting, reflections and shadows. No guide bitmap is used as visible albedo or scene imagery. The motif/depth remain an interpretation, not exact recovered ornament. Full prompts, source, limitations and build commands are in assets/ranma-relief-v01/provenance.json and tools/build-ranma-relief-field.py.

Existing lantern sources now use inverse-square decay without abrupt radius cutoff. Near lantern/hero stone-light shadow maps are fixed for the static modeled scene. Source intensities were reduced after the first full image produced over-bright paving highlights.

The raindrop-wave surface uses dark water absorption, IOR 1.333, sub-millimetre crest heights and lower coverage. The former opaque pale ring no longer reads as a white circle on the stone.

This native still and true detail subfrustum are source-hash recorded in report.json. Native validity is not photographic fidelity.
