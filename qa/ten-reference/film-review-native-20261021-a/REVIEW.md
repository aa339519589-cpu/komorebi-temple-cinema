# Ten-scene current-source motion checkpoint

This is a rejected-for-final-fidelity production draft, not a finished match. All ten seven-second source shots are real Three.js framebuffer captures at 960 by 960, 24 fps. The 60-second edit contains 1,440 frames and AAC audio. Nine overlaps retain the separate seven-second source clips; the complete source cut is 70 seconds.

The clip source files are preserved by commit `209e754`; exact per-file and per-scene identities are recorded in film-build.json. That artifact also records full video SHA-256 values. Canonical scene sources have continued advancing after capture, particularly scenes 03, 04, 08 and 09.

Actual source-film start, middle and end frames are shown in the adjacent board. Dominant open problems include the tall generic gallery mass in scene 03, the pale regular processional slabs in scene 04, broad infill and roof mass in scene 08, simplified statuary and foliage, and insufficient photo-level surface detail throughout.
