KOMOREBI / 木漏れ日

VISUALS
All architecture, sculpture, vegetation, ceramic tiles, ground, surfaces, procedural texture maps, sky, water, reflections and camera animation were created in JavaScript with Three.js.
No photographs, downloaded textures, HDRIs, stock models, generative images or video footage are used in the rendered scene.
The supplied reference collage is used only for visual comparison and is not loaded by the 3D program.
Three.js is MIT licensed. Its license is included at vendor/LICENSE.
