VISUALS
Scene06 central anatomical base: Head of Buddha, The Fralin Museum of Art; 3D data by Matthew Schneider and Will Rourk, University of Virginia Library. CC0 1.0. Dataset DOI: 10.18130/V3/NCPOAE. Original mesh, primary metadata, checksum, transformations and editable casting scripts are included. Geometry only; vertex RGB and textures are unused. This is a different historical artifact used as a sculpting base, not the photographed statue.
The active project is an unaccepted reconstruction in progress, not a completed film.
Earlier versions used only JavaScript-generated visual geometry and maps. The user explicitly authorized image generation, Blender and other tools on 2026-10-18.
Scene05 now includes source-built Blender bearing timbers, half-lapped arms and actual gilt relief geometry derived from a non-metric image-generated sculptural guide. The exact prompt, guide, builder, editable native project and real render evidence are included.
Scene05 also uses the CC0 Lacquered Cherry Wood PBR maps by Poly Haven: Jenelle van Heerden (Scanning), Rico Cilliers (Processing).
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/lacquered_cherry_wood
License: https://polyhaven.com/license
The current scene code modifies material tint, exterior-coating roughness and member-aligned UVs. Original map bytes and checksums are retained.
Scene04's latest material pass uses three built-in image-generated surface studies for cedar, flagstone granite and charcoal mineral microdetail. Original outputs, exact prompts, source hashes and the browser-side neutral albedo/height/roughness conversion are retained in reconstructions/scene-04/assets/generated-materials-20261019/ and its loader. They provide surface detail on the independently modeled geometry; they are not scene images.
The supplied reference photographs are visual-comparison inputs and are not loaded by a 3D scene. No photograph or generated replacement frame is presented as an actual modeled scene.
An additional CC0 sculpture scan published by the Minneapolis Institute of Art is retained as a separate, unselected anatomical study; it is not installed in the ten reference scenes. See THIRD_PARTY.md and its provenance files.
Three.js is MIT licensed. Its license is included at vendor/LICENSE.
