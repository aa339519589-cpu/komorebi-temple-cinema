# Local explicit nasal geometry, v04

Derivative of the failed closed v03 repair, ultimately `learned-face-v02` from `8082e52`. See that source's retained TripoSG/MIT provenance; this is not a CC0 scan or a measured statue recovery. No new learned-model inference or bitmap input was used for this local repair.

The closed nose-wing caps were lowered by explicit numeric artist section curves, then compactly faired into the retained surface. The raw section coordinates and weights are editable in `build-source.py`, and the Blend preserves the unmodified v02 head and closure/cutter solids. Final topology is one untextured mesh with 228,062 triangles, zero boundary/nonmanifold edges; 105,120 unrelated original vertices retain their positions. No camera, material, light, original neck/crown/body or seat was edited.

Native nasal88 remains unaccepted: the old through-openings are gone, but paired flattened nasal masses and seams are still anatomically inaccurate. Thick pursed lips and other upstream face differences deliberately remain. Successful topology/export/rendering is not photographic acceptance.
