# Third-party materials

- **Scene06 floor surface106:** reuses the existing unchanged Poly Haven CC0 lacquered cherry diffuse and roughness maps on the real floor boards. `tools/record-floor06-scan106.py` records original hashes, map means and a constant authored brown PBR tint; it never opens the supplied temple photograph. The original provider metric dimensions were unavailable in the local record, so the chosen grain scale remains an explicit hypothesis. Clearcoat reflections remain sampled from the actual room.
- **Scene03 lotus tissue material96:** the built-in image tool generated a neutral diffuse tissue atlas without any reference-image input. Original 1254×1254 output and exact prompt are retained in `reconstructions/scene-03/assets/lotus-leaf96/` and `lookdev/lotus03-material96/imagegen-provenance.json`. `tools/normalise-lotus03-material96.py` computes a constant PBR tint to retain the preceding material's mean diffuse colour; it does not edit the atlas. This is an authored botanical surface guide on existing closed curved leaves, not a reference-scene image or measured reflectance recovery.
- **Scene04 paving material guide87:** the built-in image tool generated a neutral granite mineral diffuse study conditioned on the supplied floor reference. Original output, exact prompt and role are in `lookdev/stone-material87/`. `tools/build-image-assisted-granite87.py` creates the periodic mineral material and an authored shallow normal estimate; it is not a measured displacement scan. `tools/build-traced-stone04-maps.py --image-assisted87` assigns this material to independent real stone solids. No assembled-floor image or reference RGB projection supplies the scene layout or its shadows.
- **Scene04 foreground scan geometry:** four foreground stones reuse the already credited Kless Gyzen / Poly Haven CC0 Rock Moss Set 01, with original scan UVs and PBR maps, uniformly scaled geometry and a documented neutral mineral tint. Their helper and source dependencies are inside `reconstructions/scene-04/`. The 31 paving stones, observed joint coordinates, curb cuts and inferred rear tree are independently authored geometry.
- **Scene04 generated PBR detail, 2026-10-19:** the built-in image tool generated neutralized cedar-lacquer, warm granite and charcoal mineral microtexture inputs. Exact prompts, original 1254×1254 files, hashes and input-reference scope are recorded in `reconstructions/scene-04/assets/generated-materials-20261019/provenance.json`. Browser code derives neutral albedo modulation, shallow microheight and roughness maps at load time and applies them to the existing three-dimensional architecture and independently modeled flagstones; neither the photographs nor a generated scene frame is loaded by the scene. Seam continuity was requested in the prompts and still requires actual rendered inspection.
- **Archived granite material trial:** Dimitrios Savva's CC0 [Granite Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_wall), original bytes and SHA256 in `reconstructions/scene-04/assets/rough-granite-wall/provenance.json`. Preserved as an unselected material experiment; the current stone material guide is separately documented above.

## Additional parallel reconstruction materials, 2026-10-18

- Scene04 paving uses Amal Kumar’s CC0 [Monastery Stone Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/monastery_stone_floor), physical width1.8m. Downloaded originals, interior crops and SHA256 provenance are retained inside `reconstructions/scene-04/assets/monastery-stone-floor/`. A single stone interior supplies the face grain; actual modeled paving joints remain geometry.
- Scenes01,02,03,07,08 now also use the already credited Poly Haven lacquered cherry scan with member-aligned UVs and separately tuned coating/colour. Source maps remain CC0; local provenance accompanies the derivatives.
- Physical lighting studies use Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest’s CC0 [Qwantani Sunset Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_sunset_puresky). The original 2K EXR and its SHA256 are retained in `assets/polyhaven/qwantani-sunset-puresky/`. It supplies measured world radiance and reflections; it contains no temple or reference-image geometry.
- Scene01 garden stones use Kless Gyzen’s CC0 [Rock Moss Set 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01). The six separate scanned meshes and original 4K PBR maps are retained with provider MD5 and local SHA256 checksums in `assets/polyhaven/rock-moss-set-01/`. Stones are individually placed and shaded in 3D; squat scan proportions are preserved, and the coating tint is modified.
- An additional physical sky study uses Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest’s CC0 [Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky). The unchanged 2K EXR and provenance are in `assets/polyhaven/kloppenheim-06-puresky/`.
- The unselected anatomical study in `lookdev/makehuman-buddha-study/source/` uses MakeHuman Community’s base mesh and Asian male target. MakeHuman’s [official license](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html) and the complete included `LICENSE.md` identify the base mesh and targets as CC0 graphic assets. The software’s separate code license does not apply to these graphic files.
- **Open depth-field reconstruction:** [Depth Anything V2 Small](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2) by Lihe Yang and collaborators. Both the selected Small checkpoint and its code are Apache-2.0; the complete license, upstream README, model card, checkpoint hash, inferred depth and camera assumptions are documented in `reconstructions/depth-field/`. Other model sizes are not used. The authored backprojection/resampling code makes actual anisotropic 3D Gaussian fields, with visible-surface geometry and photographic lighting baked into their radiance. Hidden surfaces and relightable PBR materials are not recovered. The project MIT grant does not relicense the underlying reference-image rights in the derived PLYs.
- **Spark 2.3.1:** MIT, with its source bundle, source map and license vendored under `reconstructions/sharp-study-viewer/vendor/spark/`; the included fflate component retains its MIT license. The renderer uses the project's bundled Three.js r186.
- **SHARP method comparison:** the isolated `reconstructions/sharp-study/` study retains Apple's original software license and research-model agreement. Its weights are not bundled. The model agreement limits model use to research and excludes product development. SHARP is kept as an isolated technical comparison; the `depth-field-film` delivery pipeline uses independently generated Apache Small depth fields, without SHARP predictions, cameras or PLYs.
- `lookdev/buddha-face-study/` preserves an **unselected** image-assisted skin scaffold study. The canonical468-vertex topology comes from Google MediaPipe0.10.21 under Apache2.0; its complete license is included in that directory. Inferred depth is not a metric scan and the generated clay image is not a scene albedo or a replacement frame. Exact generation prompt, original reference crop, intermediate geometry and actual Blender views are preserved.

- The added Three.js r186 EXR loader retains its full Syoyo Fujita and Industrial Light & Magic BSD notices. Its fflate dependency retains its MIT header. Exact upstream URLs and checksums are in `vendor/loader-provenance.json`.
- Scene04 now also uses Poly Haven’s CC0 Granite Tile 04, by Amal Kumar; original maps, interior face crop and provider attribution are retained in `reconstructions/scene-04/assets/granite-tile-04/provenance.json`. Scene10 reuses only this local stone surface grain plus the already credited independent Rock Moss Set meshes.
